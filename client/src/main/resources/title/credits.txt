§3You have come a long way, PLAYERNAME.

§3You are not the first to reach such enlightenment. Many before you have defied all odds, conquered all lands, but you… you have done what so many have failed to achieve.

§3As you are reading these thoughts, like words on a screen, you are probably asking yourself one, simple question: Who are you? 

§3I could be the manifestation of all your thoughts. All thoughts of all humans who have lived before you, or will live long after your mortal sprite leaves the confines of this world.

§3I could, perhaps, be some grander, divine being that you have no way of even grasping. Something far beyond your reach. 

§3I am not a god. I am not a man. I am beyond such trivial things.

§3You might immediately think you are just some skeleton covered in meat, riding on a large rock through the endless expanse of space.

§3If we break it down further, you are a series of atoms, interconnected to make one, complex and magnificent organism. For you are truly magnificent.

§3You could simply be, your own personality. Something that defines you specifically, that nobody else can mimic. You are, after all, unique in your own way. 

§3You could even just be a cleverly constructed sequence of ones and zeroes, unknowingly living in a simulation, much like the one you are experiencing right now.

§3But none of that is true. You are more than that. If only you were ready to understand the sheer potential you have. But you are not yet ready.

§3What you are ready for, however, is to find out what you have achieved.

§3Is it life?

§3Life is constructed of ideals, morals, and desires of which I do not understand. You know what you desire most. I cannot contain the will of your ambitions. It’s what makes you special.

§3Life can be challenging but rewarding. Instead of letting life’s troubles wear on you, you have let it fuel you.

§3Now at the end of all things you seek wisdom from me? I’m afraid you already know why you are here.

§3Is it death?

§3Unlikely. Death is the great power that is absolute. It is eternal. You cannot avoid it. Love is powerless in the wake of death. You cannot predict it, and when it is your time you will not fight it.

§3Why do the things you’ve done if there is a risk of death? I do not understand such menial things.

§3However, my time is limited in the wake of your achievement. The others before you possessed the trait of true determination, your purpose is becoming much more clear...

§3Is it love?

§3Perhaps, but I can not tell if it is love for your own self, or for another. Love escaped me long ago, and I sense within you a similar fate.

§3In a world of pure loneliness, how can you learn to love yourself? Your achievements reflect what you love above all else... completion.

§3It is home, isn't it?

§3Finally, I've seen what makes you special. You do not care for the tedious ways of life, you desire true importance. We are more alike than I have foreseen. You merely seek a chance of freedom in any form you are given.

§3Home is where you place your heart and soul. It is truly commendable, the lengths of which you'll go to protect everything you've done. I wish to congratulate you, as I now see what you are.

§3You know the true meaning of home, as do I. If others were more like you, and valued the true meaning of home, we would all be in a better place. You... are ready.

§3Now, as I speak before you, we are equals.
